Privacy Policy for DISC Focus

Package ID: com.aman.disc • Developer: Aman Dahiya • Effective Date: November 25, 2026

DISC Focus ("DISC", "we", "our") is a device-first focus timer, study planner, and distraction blocker built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what data DISC accesses, how it is used, and how you control it.

1. Data Processed Strictly On-Device (Never Uploaded)

  • Accessibility Service API: DISC uses Android's Accessibility Service solely on your device to detect when you open a user-selected blocked app, YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, or a user-blocked website URL in mobile browsers, and to enforce active Focus Lock / Permanent Lock protections. DISC does not collect, store remotely, sell, or share screen content, keystrokes, passwords, or browsing history.
  • Usage Access (Screen Time): Used strictly on-device to show your daily screen time and enforce your chosen Daily App Limits.
  • Study Proof Photos: Optional task proof photos are verified locally on-device using ML Kit text/detail checks and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync

You can use DISC completely offline in Guest Mode with zero cloud storage. If you choose to tap Continue with Google, DISC uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to store only:

  • Your Google Account UID, display name, and email (for authentication).
  • Your optional cloud backup of focus sessions, streak count, streak savers, and study task titles/deadlines.
  • Your live focus status, daily focus minutes, and reactions inside Streakers study groups you explicitly create or join.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not show third-party behavioral ads.

3. Data Deletion & Control

You can delete your cloud backup directly inside the app under Settings → Delete Cloud Backup at any time, or sign out to stay 100% local. To request complete deletion of your account and all cloud data, email amandahiya.sp.office@gmail.com with the subject "DISC Data Deletion Request" and we will permanently delete your records within 7 days.

4. Children's Privacy

DISC is intended for students and general productivity users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Aman Dahiya at amandahiya.sp.office@gmail.com.
